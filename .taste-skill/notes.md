# Notes — Weave landing page

## Inventory (only Weave has these)
1. The name: Weave, one thread through the day ("Your whole day, on one thread").
2. The mark: a yellow square whose top-right corner lifts as a black quarter circle (locked, docs/logo/kit).
3. Brand colours: yellow #F5C84C, ink #141414. Font: Geist (Medium, Regular).
4. Up next: today and later this week, the first screen of the app.
5. Reminders that ring on the minute (alarm-clock mode on Android).
6. Local first: everything is saved on the device, works with no signal.
7. Sync between phone, Mac and PC; if two devices edit, both changes are kept.
8. The mini bar: a small bar floating above every app on Mac and Windows.
9. List colours (blue, green, purple, yellow...), Archive, Trash and Undo.
10. No ads, no tracking, delete the account in Settings.
11. Subtitle chosen for the App Store: "Remember less. Do more."
12. Store state: beta (TestFlight, Play testing); no public store links yet.

## Argument (claim → proof)
- Hero: Weave remembers for you → the line "Remember less. Do more." with the real Up next card in it; action: join the beta.
- Try it → a real working list on the page, saved in this browser (offline, like the app).
- What it does → switch sentences: on-time reminders, offline, sync, mini bar, repeats; each with its consequence.
- On every screen → real renders: phone Up next, Mac window, mini bar sheet, dark mode.
- Yours → no ads, no tracking, local first, delete anytime; link to the privacy policy.
- Get it → phone or computer? two beta buttons.
- Footer → name, privacy, support.

## Direction
sentence-as-layout (seed 1373249346). Why: Weave already owns a short sentence ("Remember less. Do more.") and its product is software,
so the slot inside the sentence can hold the app's own Up next card: the page reads as the store line with the proof inside it.

## Idea
On this page, Weave's lifted corner becomes the inline frame inside the sentence: a yellow tile in the headline whose black corner
lifts to show the real Up next card. Competitor test: no other to-do app has this mark; the sentence fails with Todoist in it.

## Live thing
"Try a list": the headline input adds real rows to a small working list (tick, undo), kept in this browser's storage,
so it survives a reload like Weave survives no signal. Fallback without script: a static list.

## Redraws / deviations
- Hero seam: the seam runs between the two lines, not through the letters (the inline card cannot be clipped across two inks cleanly).
- Section 3 (second "headline you can type") replaced: one live input per page; see alternatives.
- About: no portrait exists; the drop cap is the Weave mark itself.
