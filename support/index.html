Contact us

Write to mobiledev.mayank@gmail.com for questions, problems or ideas.

If something isn't working, it helps to tell us:

  • your device (for example iPhone, Android phone, Mac or Windows PC),
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Please don't send passwords or the text of private tasks. We never need them.

Delete your account

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account. This removes your account, your synced lists and tasks, and your device list from our server.
  • Without the app: email mobiledev.mayank@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my Weave account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Full details: Keeping and deleting your data in the privacy policy.

Get Weave

Weave is in beta.

  • iPhone and Mac: join the TestFlight beta.
  • Android: get it on Google Play.
  • Windows: not ready yet. Email us and we'll tell you when it is.

Crash reports

If the app runs into an error, it sends us a crash report so we can fix it. It never includes your tasks, lists or email. To turn it off: Settings → Crash reports → Send crash reports. More in the privacy policy.
