Weave keeps your lists on your device first, and works without an account. Sign in with your email only if you want sync. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

To sync, we store your email, your changes and the names of your devices. It isn’t end-to-end encrypted, and you can delete it all from Settings. The privacy policy has the details.
