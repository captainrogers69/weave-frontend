Weave

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Weave is a lists and tasks app for iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows, made by Mayank Mobile Dev ("we"). This policy explains what data Weave handles and why.

In short

  • Without an account, everything stays on your device. We receive nothing.
  • With an account, your lists and tasks are stored on our server so your devices can sync.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and all its data from the app at any time.

Using Weave without an account

Weave works fully offline. Your lists, tasks, due dates and reminders are saved only in the app's storage on your device. Reminders are scheduled by your device's own notification system. Nothing is sent to us.

Using Weave with an account

Signing in is optional and turns on sync between your devices. When you sign in, we store:

DataWhy
Your email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider, never in plain text.
Your lists and tasks (names, text, due dates, reminders, repeat settings and order)To sync them to your other signed-in devices.
Each signed-in device's name, type (for example iPhone or Windows) and when it last syncedTo show your devices in Settings and label edits made on different devices.

This data is used only to provide sync. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and it is not shared with anyone except the service providers below.

We process this data because it is needed to provide the sync service you sign up for (performance of a contract). It is stored on servers in Mumbai, India. If you live in the European Economic Area or the UK, this means your data is transferred outside it, and Supabase protects such transfers with Standard Contractual Clauses.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts the account and sync service (database and authentication). It processes the data above on our behalf. See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • App stores. When you download Weave, Apple, Google or Microsoft may collect data under their own privacy policies. We don't control that data.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics, usage tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • No crash reporting services.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.

Security

Data travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On the server, each account can read only its own data. We plan to add end-to-end encryption in a future version, and will update this policy when we do.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep your account data while your account exists.
  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account. This removes your account, your synced lists and tasks, and your device list from our server.
  • Sign out to remove your synced data from a device. Your account stays on the server.
  • Data that exists only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion.
  • After deletion, copies may remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for up to 30 days, until those backups are overwritten. They are not used or restored in that time.

Delete your account without the app: email mobiledev.mayank@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my Weave account". We delete the account, its synced lists and tasks, and its device list within 30 days and confirm by email. Nothing else is kept, apart from the temporary backups described above and anything the law requires us to keep.

Children

Weave is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is processed. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app.
